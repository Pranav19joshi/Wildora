Wildora is a Ramnagar travel desk.

We are starting out. No review wall or photo reel yet. What we have is two people you can call, and a town that sits at the Corbett gate.

The desk

Two advisors. One town.
No call centre.

Most travel desks planning Uttarakhand trips are based in Delhi or Noida. We live in Ramnagar — the town at the southern gate of Jim Corbett. We know which lodges are genuinely close to the Bijrani gate and which ones charge a Corbett premium for a location that is an hour from the forest.

For Char Dham, Sanskar has done the road personally and knows the altitude-pacing mistakes that most itineraries get wrong. For Nainital and the Kumaon belt, the Kaladhungi route from Ramnagar passes Jim Corbett's winter home — the kind of thing you know when you drive it regularly.

We will put photos here when we have our own. Until then, WhatsApp is the working inbox and both numbers are real people, not a chatbot.

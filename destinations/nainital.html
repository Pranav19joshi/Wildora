Nainital

The lake hill-station of Kumaon. Naini Lake, Snow View, Mall Road, and some of the best birding in the Himalayan foothills. About 2 hours from Ramnagar.

What to do in 2–4 days

Naini Lake

The pear-shaped lake the town is built around. Rowing and pedal boats available all day from the Tallital and Mallital jetties. Evenings are best — cooler, lit up, and less crowded than midday.

Snow View & Tiffin Top

Snow View Point at 2,270 m — ropeway from Mallital or a 30-minute walk. On clear winter mornings you see Nanda Devi, Nanda Kot, and Trishul. Tiffin Top is a 4 km walk through oak forest ending at a meadow viewpoint.

Naina Devi Temple

One of the 51 Shakti Peethas, at the north end of the lake. Short walk from Mall Road. Go early morning to avoid the midday crowd.

Mall Road

Closed to private vehicles, 3.5 km end to end. Old shops, restaurants, the colonial-era library, and the boat jetties. The evening promenade is the best version of it.

Mukteshwar (day excursion)

51 km from Nainital at 2,286 m, about 1.5 hours. A quieter ridge with apple orchards and the Mukteshwar Mahadev temple perched on a cliff edge. Add this if you have a free day.

Pangot & Kilbury (birding)

6 km from town on the Nainital–Almora road. The top birding location in Kumaon — Kalij pheasant, Himalayan woodpeckers, winter visitors. Worth half a day for anyone with binoculars.

Getting from Ramnagar

Ramnagar to Nainital via Kaladhungi is about 65 km, roughly 2 hours. The route passes through Kaladhungi, where Jim Corbett's winter home is now a small museum — worth a quick stop. We arrange a taxi for the day or the full trip.

From Delhi by rail: Kathgodam station (23 km below Nainital) is the railhead. Shatabdi from New Delhi or Kathgodam Express from Old Delhi. Taxi from Kathgodam to Nainital takes about 1 hour.

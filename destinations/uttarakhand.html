Wider Uttarakhand

Chopta, Auli, Valley of Flowers, Lansdowne, Kausani — when one destination is not enough, or when you want somewhere less visited.

Places in this category

Chopta – Tungnath – Chandrashila

Tungnath is the highest Shiva temple in the world at 3,680 m. A 3.5 km trek from Chopta (2,680 m) through bugyal meadows. Spectacular in April (rhododendrons) and October (clear skies). Chandrashila summit at 4,130 m is a further 1 km. Basic guesthouses at Chopta. Best in May and September–October.

Auli & Joshimath

Auli at 2,519 m is India's best ski resort — 4 km run and Asia's longest gondola. In summer, the meadows give unobstructed Nanda Devi views without crowds. Joshimath below is both the Auli base and the winter seat of the Badrinath temple deity.

Valley of Flowers & Hemkund Sahib

UNESCO World Heritage site in Chamoli district. 16 km trek from Govindghat. Open mid-July to mid-September. The entire valley floor blooms with 300+ Himalayan wildflowers. Hemkund Sahib (4,329 m), a Sikh holy lake, is 6 km from the Valley. Both in a 3-day circuit from Ghangaria.

Lansdowne

Garhwal Rifles cantonment town at 1,706 m. 258 km from Delhi. Very few tourists. Pine forest, Tip-in-Top viewpoint, Bhulla Lake, and a small army museum. Genuinely quiet in a way most Uttarakhand hill-stations are not. 2 nights is enough.

Kausani

1,890 m, Kumaon district. A narrow ridge with 300 km of Himalayan panorama on a clear day: Trishul, Nanda Devi, Panchachuli. Gandhi's Anasakti Ashram is here. A nearby tea estate. 2–3 nights. Works as an extension to a Nainital trip.

Ranikhet

Cantonment town at 1,829 m, 50 km from Almora. Golf course (one of the highest in Asia), Chaubatia Apple Orchards, and the Kumaon Regimental Centre Museum. Low tourist footprint. Good extension after Nainital.

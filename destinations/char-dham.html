Char Dham

Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri, Yamunotri. A pilgrimage circuit through the high Himalaya — planned properly so the altitude doesn't ruin it.

The four shrines

Yamunotri

Source of the Yamuna. 5 km trek from Janki Chatti on a stone path up to the hot spring kunds and the temple at 3,291 m. The gentlest of the four physically.

Gangotri

Source of the Bhagirathi, which becomes the Ganga. Temple at 3,048 m. Most pilgrims also walk the extra 19 km to Gaumukh, the glacier snout — a separate day.

Kedarnath

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas. Temple at 3,583 m, backed by permanent snowfield. 16 km trek from Gaurikund, or helicopter from Phata / Sersi / Guptkashi. The most demanding — altitude, cold, and crowd management in peak weeks all need planning well in advance.

Badrinath

Dedicated to Vishnu, at 3,133 m. The most accessible — road reaches the temple gate. Tapt Kund hot spring is 50 metres below. Mana village, the last Indian settlement before the Tibetan border, is a 3 km walk worth doing.

Typical 10-day road plan

Day 1–2: Haridwar / Rishikesh — arrival, acclimatise. Day 3: Drive to Barkot, visit Yamunotri. Day 4: Drive to Uttarkashi. Day 5: Visit Gangotri, return to Uttarkashi. Day 6: Drive to Guptkashi. Day 7: Kedarnath trek or helicopter, overnight at the top. Day 8: Darshan, descend to Guptkashi, drive toward Badrinath. Day 9: Badrinath darshan, Mana village. Day 10: Drive to Haridwar, depart.

On helicopters: Kedarnath helicopter slots (Phata, Sersi, Guptkashi) book out weeks ahead in peak season. We book through authorised operators only — no resellers. If someone offers last-minute slots at inflated rates online, that is almost always a reseller.

Registration

Uttarakhand government requires biometric yatra registration before the circuit. Registration counters are at Haridwar, Rishikesh, and each Dham. Carry original government ID. Share a copy of your Aadhaar when you enquire and we coordinate the registration alongside your travel planning.

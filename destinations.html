Where we send people

Five places we work in regularly. Durations and seasons are typical, not a fixed promise — gates, snow, and yatra dates shift every year. We track them and advise you before you book.

2–5 days · November – June

Jim Corbett

India's oldest national park, 15 km from Ramnagar. Bengal tiger safaris, sal forest birding, and five distinct zones each with its own character. Our home base gives us a real logistics edge here.

8–12 days · May–Jun, Sep–Oct

Char Dham

Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri, Yamunotri. A road yatra that takes 8–12 days done properly. We handle biometric registrations, GMVN halts, and helicopter slots through authorised operators.

Plan a Char Dham yatra →
2–4 days · Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov

Nainital

Kumaon's lake hill-station at 2,084 m. Naini Lake, Snow View ropeway, Naina Devi temple, Mall Road, and a Mukteshwar day excursion if you have time. About 2 hours from Ramnagar.

Plan a Nainital stay →
2–4 days · Year-round · Rafting Sep–Jun

Rishikesh

Triveni Ghat aarti, Shivpuri white-water rafting, the Beatles Ashram, riverside yoga retreats, and the natural first or last stop on any Char Dham road plan.

Corbett safaris

Jeep and canter permits across five zones — Bijrani, Dhikala, Jhirna, Dhela, Durga Devi. We book the permit, the vehicle, and a lodge near the gate you're actually entering from. No detours, no hour-long drives before the forest starts.

02

Char Dham

Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri, Yamunotri — 8 to 12 days if you pace it properly. Road plan, GMVN halts, biometric yatra registration, and helicopter slots when they are available and make sense.

03

Hill stays & family trips

Nainital, Rishikesh, Chopta, Auli, Lansdowne — rooms that sleep 3–4, taxis that know hill roads, and days paced for children and older parents. No over-ambitious 14-hour itineraries.

Things people always ask

No. Nobody can — not us, not the best guide in the park. What we do is book you into a zone where sightings are more consistent (Bijrani and Dhikala are historically stronger), time your safari for early morning when animals are most active, and put you in a lodge near the right gate so you're not losing 40 minutes before you even enter the forest.
For weekends between November and February, 3–4 weeks minimum. Dhikala Forest Rest House — the overnight stay inside the core zone — books out 45 days in advance on the day slots open. For weekday safaris in the shoulder months (March–June), 10–14 days is usually fine. Tell us your dates and we will check what is realistic before you book anything.
The yatra typically opens around Akshaya Tritiya (April–May) and closes after Diwali (October–November). The exact dates change every year — announced a few weeks prior based on the Hindu calendar and weather. Roads above Joshimath and Guptkashi close first when winter snow arrives. We track these dates each season and advise you before you book travel.
By train: the Ranikhet Express (15013/15014) from Old Delhi station arrives at Ramnagar in about 6–7 hours overnight — the comfortable option. By road from Delhi it is about 5–6 hours depending on Moradabad traffic. We arrange pickup from Ramnagar station or from the highway if you are driving.

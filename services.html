Home / What we arrange

Permits, rooms, and a driver who shows up.

We are not a hotel brand and we do not own jeeps. We book the pieces and stay on the phone while you are on the road.

01

Jungle safari permits & gypsies

Corbett has five main zones — Bijrani, Dhikala, Jhirna, Dhela, Durga Devi. Availability changes by season, and some zones require more lead time than others. We advise on which zone fits your dates, book the government-authorised jeep or canter, and pair it with a lodge near the right entry gate.

02

Stays & forest rest houses

For Corbett, Forest Rest Houses inside the park are the closest you get to the wildlife — and they book out fastest. We know the lead times and try for them first if you have flexibility. Outside the park, we choose lodges for location first (near your safari gate), then budget. We say when a "luxury resort" is not worth what it charges.

03

Char Dham logistics

Biometric yatra registration, GMVN guesthouse bookings at each halt, road plan timing (Badrinath and Kedarnath gates have daily opening windows), and helicopter slot booking through authorised operators. We know which days get congested and which routes are better taken at dawn.

04

Mountain transport & drivers

Hill roads need drivers who know hill roads. We use local drivers from Ramnagar and the Kumaon–Garhwal belt — people who know which section of the Badrinath highway blocks first, where to stop for lunch without getting overcharged, and how to handle a single-lane cliff road. Tempo Traveller for groups; Innova for 4–5 people.

05

Family & senior travel

Slower days. Rooms that sleep 3–4. Buffer days built in for delayed trains to Ramnagar or Kathgodam, or for altitude sickness that sometimes takes a day to pass. We flag any itinerary section that is hard on elderly knees or unsuitable for children under 6 before you book.

06

On-trip support

You keep both phone numbers. If a safari zone is suddenly closed, a road gets blocked, or a stay falls through — we reshuffle rather than leaving you with a screenshot and an apology. We do not guarantee we can fix everything, but we stay available while you are on the road.
